Controls

Click a cell to drop the box with its near-left-bottom corner there. It falls onto whatever is underneath and must rest on at least 80% of its own base, so only placements a palletiser would not trust are refused — any stable cell is yours, not just the corners of the empty maximal spaces. The packer is confined to those corners, so the purple cells are placements it could never have played. The agent never sees more of the conveyor than you do.
